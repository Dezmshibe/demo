How it works

  1. Send the details

    Date, venue, headcount and budget range. Rough numbers are fine.

  2. Get a quote

    You'll receive an itemised quote within one working day.

  3. Taste and confirm

    For events over 80 guests we arrange a tasting before you commit.

  4. We handle the day

    Setup, service and clear-down, with a named event lead on site.
